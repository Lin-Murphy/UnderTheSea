Ultrasound Wonders Voiceover Script — Under the Sea (确定版, 2026-11-05)
Cue 对应:文件 → 阶段 / 触发(见 Assets/Editor/BuildReefMainScene.cs 的 vo.cues)

VO_01  Opening — Enter,VR 完全显现后 1s(与开场背景乐同时)
  Welcome to Ultrasound Wonders: Under the Sea.
  Make yourself comfortable. Here, you are a coral, resting gently beneath the waves.
  For now, breathe normally. Follow your clinician's instructions, and let them know if you need a pause.

VO_02  Gel — ApplyGel,进入后 1s
  A gentle wave passes over you.
  Some little fish have come to say hello.

VO_03  Probe contact — Probe,探头 tag 第一次进入海草区域后 0.3s(3s 内没识别到也照常播放)
  (2026-11-05 晚新录音,台词略改;旧的 VO_03.1 / VO_03.2 不再使用)
  A little clownfish nestles close as the seagrass gently rests against you.
  You may feel a little pressure.

VO_04  Night falls — DuskTransition,进入后 0.5s(2026-11-05 晚新录音,台词略改)
  Night is falling over the reef, the little fish nestle among your coral branches.
  Let's help the jellyfish's gentle light grow.

VO_05  Breathe in — BreathHold,进入后 1s;只播 0–5.72s(2026-11-06 去掉 "Watch the jellyfish grow and glow.")
  Take a slow breath in, as deeply as feels comfortable.

场景4 呼吸引导(BreathGuide,2026-11-06):
  05 播完后按住扳机 → 吸气声;吸气 1s 后视觉中心缓现字幕 "Hold"
  松开扳机 → "Hold" 缓慢消失 → 配音 VO_06 5.49–7.29s:
  Now you can breathe normally.
  → 接吐气声。每次按/松都重复。
  VO_06 其它句子(Gently hold… / Perfect. / Let's try it again… / Wonderful…)不再使用。

VO_07  Resume normal breathing — ExhaleTransition(按 A 天亮),进入后 0.5s
  You can breathe normally now.
  The light spreads softly around the little fish.

VO_08  Closing — Exit,进入后 0.3s;回到现实后继续播完(只有按 B 才中断)
  Our underwater journey is coming to an end.
  Thank you for sharing this little ocean with us.
  Gently bring your attention back to the room. Keep resting while we help you remove the headset.
